# Taiwan Traditional Chinese and punctuation

Read this together with `deai-voice.md` when Taiwan terminology or Chinese
punctuation needs judgment.

## Terminology

- Default to terms that a Taiwan reader would normally expect when there is a
  clear everyday or technical equivalent.
- Judge the meaning before replacing a term. Keep field-specific language,
  quotations, and wording the user deliberately chose.
- Prefer naming the actual action or outcome over vague internet or business
  shorthand. These are contextual preferences, not a blanket replacement list.

## Punctuation

Use full-width punctuation in Chinese prose. Do not normalize punctuation
inside code, URLs, identifiers, commands, or English-only spans. When Chinese
and English meet in ordinary prose, keep the surrounding Chinese punctuation
consistent with the sentence.
