# Translation into Traditional Chinese

Read this together with `deai-voice.md` before translating.

## Fidelity comes first

Preserve terminology, technical meaning, evidence strength, register, and
deliberate rhetoric in the source. Do not remove a purposeful contrast,
parallel construction, or formal turn of phrase merely because ordinary
de-AI guidance would avoid it.

## Make the target text natural only after fidelity is safe

- Split overloaded modifier chains when doing so preserves the relation among
  the ideas.
- Prefer an active or agentless Chinese sentence when a passive form adds no
  meaning.
- Remove pronouns that Chinese can safely omit, but retain references needed
  to avoid ambiguity.
- Reorder clauses when natural Chinese flow makes the same logic clearer.

If a source term, ambiguity, or wordplay cannot be carried across faithfully,
state the limit or ask instead of inventing a cleaner meaning.
